# LiveFit Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [date of publishing]_

LiveFit is a workout tracker for runs, walks and strength training. This policy explains what the app collects, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

**In short:** everything you record stays on your phone. LiveFit has no accounts and no servers, and it never sells or shares your data.

## What LiveFit stores

All of the following is stored **only on your device**, in the app's private storage:

- **Profile:** the name you enter (optional), your units, your weight (optional, used for calorie estimates), your weekly goal and your app settings.
- **Runs and walks:** the GPS positions recorded during each workout (with time, accuracy and speed), pause times, laps, and the totals worked out from them (distance, time, pace), plus any title and notes you add.
- **Strength workouts:** the exercises, sets, reps, weights and durations you log, and any custom exercises you create.

## Location

- LiveFit uses your location **only while you are recording a run or walk**. You start and stop recording yourself.
- While recording, Android shows a notification ("LiveFit is recording your run"), so you always know location is in use, even with the screen off.
- LiveFit does not ask for "Allow all the time" location access and does not track your location at any other time.
- Your routes are not uploaded anywhere.

## Notifications

LiveFit can show local notifications that are created on your phone: the recording notification, rest-timer alerts between sets, and workout reminders on the days you choose. Nothing is sent from a server. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings.

## Crash reports

If LiveFit crashes, a crash report may be sent to Sentry (sentry.io), a crash-reporting service, so the problem can be fixed. A report contains technical details only: the error and where in the code it happened, the app version, the phone model and Android version.

Crash reports do **not** include your location, routes, workouts, notes, name or weight, and LiveFit does not send your IP address or any personal details with them. Sentry processes this data for LiveFit as a service provider.

## What LiveFit does not do

- No account and no sign-in.
- No advertising and no advertising IDs.
- No analytics or tracking of how you use the app.
- No selling, renting or sharing of your data with anyone.

## Keeping and deleting your data

- Your data stays on your phone until you delete it.
- You can delete any workout in the app (open it, then use the bin icon).
- Uninstalling LiveFit, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything it has stored.
- Because nothing is kept on a server, there is nothing else to delete.

## Children

LiveFit is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version works differently (for example, an optional account with cloud backup), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
